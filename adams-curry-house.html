Taj's Local Tips: Adams Curry House

First-hand recommendations from a regular local customer. These are personal experiences, not verified current offers or guarantees. Please check details when ordering.

For a curry closer to home cooking

One regular customer rates Adams as their favourite local place for curries, describing the flavour as delicious and closer to authentic home-cooked curry. They particularly like that the usual chicken and meat curry meals they've ordered have been boneless. If you need boneless meat, confirm when ordering.

A curry meal for around £8.50

They report getting a curry with a choice of two chapatis, rice, chips or one naan for around £8.50. They also appreciate that, in their experience, salad and sauces have been included without an extra charge — a great accompaniment to the curry. Confirm the current meal options, price and extras before ordering.

Ask about the staff handi

For something especially traditional, ask whether there's a staff handi available. The customer says Adams sometimes makes extra of the curry cooked for its staff and will sell portions to customers, in their experience for the same price as a regular curry. Important: unlike the boneless curries they usually order, a staff handi may contain chicken or meat on the bone. Ask what's been cooked that day, whether any is available and what it costs.

A handi is a traditional style of curry, often cooked in a deep pot. Staff meals and recipes can vary; availability is not guaranteed.
