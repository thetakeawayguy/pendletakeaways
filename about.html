About Pendle Takeaways

Pendle Takeaways is an independent directory project focused on food and takeaway information in Nelson, Colne and the wider Pendle area.

Our aim is to make local food information easier to find, with plain-English explanations of dishes and links to listings as they are developed.

This directory is not an ordering service. A listing does not imply that a business has endorsed or partnered with us. Always confirm menus, prices, opening hours and allergy requirements directly with the business.
