Understanding the food

If you are looking at a South Asian sweets or savouries counter for the first time, these explanations may help. They describe food types generally and do not imply current availability at Mansha.

Samosa: Crispy pastry filled with spiced potato, meat or vegetables.

Pakora: Battered and fried snack, often made with vegetables, chicken or fish.

Seekh kebab: Spiced minced meat cooked in a long sausage shape.

Chaat: A savoury snack bowl with yoghurt, chutney, chickpeas, potatoes and crispy bits.

Mithai: South Asian sweets, often made with milk, sugar, nuts, syrup or flour.

Jalebi: Bright orange spiral sweet, crispy outside and syrupy inside.

Gulab jamun: Soft round sweet soaked in syrup.

Barfi: Dense milk-based sweet, usually cut into squares or diamonds.

Rasmalai: Soft milk dumplings served in sweet creamy milk.
