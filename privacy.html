About this website

Pendle Takeaways is an independent local directory hosted using GitHub Pages. We do not currently provide accounts, online ordering or payment collection on this site.

Optional analytics

We use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-4D6EX42QQW) only when you choose “Accept analytics”. It may collect information about how you use the site, including page visits and technical/browser information, and may set cookies or similar identifiers. If you reject analytics, our site does not load the Google Analytics tag.

Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage so we can remember it. Use “Cookie preferences” at the bottom of a page to change the selection for future visits. Removing previously set Google cookies may require clearing them in your browser.

Hosting and third parties

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure pages. Clicking links to external sites means those sites handle data under their own policies. If you submit an issue through GitHub, the information you post is public and subject to GitHub's policies.

Advertising

Google AdSense advertising is not currently enabled. If advertising is introduced, this notice and the consent arrangements will be updated before adverts run.

Your choices

You can reject optional analytics without losing access to the directory. You can also adjust your browser's cookie settings. For more information about Google's data practices, see Google's Privacy Policy.
