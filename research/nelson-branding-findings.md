## Publication checks

- Link directly to an official business site or ordering page where available. Where there is no official web menu, show the verified phone/address and say that a current menu has not been confirmed.
- Do not reproduce prices from aggregator results unless the exact item and price are verified against a current ordering page and dated on the profile.
- Do not treat a source image, review, hygiene record, opening time, or third-party cuisine label as permission or as an endorsement.
- Request written permission or business-supplied files before embedding genuine logos or food photos.
- For Good Fortune, use only the corroborated address and phone until an official channel is identified; the search results contain conflicting opening-hour listings.
